Meet the doctors

Two homoeopaths, one family practice

Dr. Vivek A. Mali, BHMS
Practicing classical homoeopathy since 2009

Completed BHMS at Gandhi Natha Rangji Homoeopathic Medical College, Solapur. Began practice in Gadhinglaj, expanded to Pune from 2012 and moved full-time from March 2021 — formerly also practicing in Panjim, Goa. Known for treating chronic and complex cases: rheumatoid arthritis, thyroid disorders, uterine fibroids & breast tumours, migraines, psoriasis, infertility, asthma, nasal polyps, tonsillitis and hernias.

Dr. Swati V. Mali, BHMS
Practicing since 2010

Topped all four years of BHMS at Mahila Homoeopathic College, Solapur. Practiced in Nashik until 2015, married Dr. Vivek Mali in March 2015, then ran her own clinic in Gadhinglaj before moving to Pune full-time in March 2021. Known for meticulous case-taking and a holistic, whole-person approach — with a focus on women's and children's health.
